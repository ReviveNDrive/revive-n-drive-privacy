PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Revive 'N Drive: My Garage handles account, Garage, advertising, purchase, and diagnostic information.

Effective September 22, 2026 · Last updated October 20, 2026 · Android package com.revivendrive

Delete account or Garage data

Product separation: Revive 'N Drive is the consumer automotive app. It is separate from Mobile Mechanic Shop OS, the professional business product.

Local-first Garage

Vehicles and service records remain available on the device. Optional Google sign-in enables authenticated Firebase synchronization.

Free Library

Included field guides are free. Google Play handles the optional permanent Remove Ads purchase.

Advertising choices

Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform provide ads, consent choices, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Information we handle

Google account and cloud Garage

Google sign-in is optional. If used, Google may provide name, email, profile image, and an account identifier. We do not receive your Google password. The app can synchronize a private Garage snapshot in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under the authenticated account.

Garage information may include VIN and vehicle details, mileage, notes, maintenance and repair history, costs, parts, diagnostic observations and answers, saved references/specifications, preferences, and reading progress. Structured records are also stored locally for offline use. Local references to selected photos or receipts do not themselves upload those binary files in the current version.

Camera and files

Optional camera access scans VIN text on-device. The app does not continuously record video. Android's system picker gives access only to files you select.

VIN decoding

When you request a lookup, the VIN is sent over an encrypted connection to the U.S. NHTSA vPIC service. NHTSA may receive normal network information such as IP address. See the NHTSA privacy policy.

Advertising and identifiers

The free app displays ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Depending on device, region, consent, Google settings, and ad availability, Google may process Android advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, device/app information, ad impressions and clicks, consent signals, and diagnostic information to deliver, limit, measure, secure, and prevent fraud involving ads.

Google's User Messaging Platform provides consent and privacy options where required. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. We do not sell personal information. See Google advertising privacy information.

Google Play purchase

Revive 'N Drive offers a one-time remove_ads purchase. Google Play processes payment and maintains the entitlement. We do not receive full card or bank details. The app may receive product, price, status, time, acknowledgement/refund status, and purchase-token information needed to verify and restore access. A one-way hash—not the raw token—is stored in the local entitlement record.

The included Library guides are currently free. Revive 'N Drive+ is a future system and is not active unless explicitly offered in the app and Play listing.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide, save, synchronize, and restore Garage features; deliver maintenance, diagnostic, Library, and VIN tools; authenticate users; display and secure ads; restore Remove Ads; respond to support/deletion requests; and protect the service.

Limited information may be processed by Google Firebase and Identity, Google Mobile Ads and UMP, Google Play, NHTSA when a VIN lookup is requested, service providers needed to operate the app, authorities where legally required, or a successor organization subject to this policy. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until deleted, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Cloud Garage records remain until you select Delete Cloud Account & Garage inside the app or submit a verified request using our external page.

Open deletion instructions

Deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and cloud Garage data we control, subject to limited legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction retention. It does not delete your Google Account or Google Play transaction history.

Security, children, and your choices

We use encrypted connections, Android private storage, authenticated Firebase access, and per-user Firestore rules. No system is completely secure.

The app is not directed to children under 13. Depending on location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdrawal of consent, or a copy of information we control. You may use core local features without Google sign-in, decline the camera, manage ad privacy options, or purchase permanent ad removal.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes may receive additional notice.
