About this policy

Revive ’N Drive ("we," "us," or "our") provides Revive ’N Drive: My Garage, a consumer Android app for managing your own vehicles, maintenance and repair records, diagnostic notes, automotive reference information, and purchased digital books. It is separate from Mobile Mechanic Shop OS, the professional business product.

This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, when it is shared, and the choices available to you.

Information we handle

Google account information

If you choose to sign in with Google when account sign-in is available, Google may provide the basic account information you authorize, such as your name, email address, profile image, and a Google account identifier. We use it to identify your Revive ’N Drive account, associate eligible purchases and entitlements, restore access, and support account-related features.

We do not receive or store your Google password. Google handles authentication. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Vehicle and garage information

You may enter a VIN, vehicle details, mileage, notes, photos, documents, receipts, maintenance and repair history, diagnostic observations, trouble codes, parts, specifications, costs, preferences, and reading progress. In the current version, this data is stored locally on your device. If cloud backup or synchronization is introduced, the app will identify that feature and this policy will be updated before cloud processing begins.

Camera and selected files

Optional camera access is used to scan VIN text. Frames are analyzed on the device and are not used to record video or continuously track you. A detected VIN is shown for confirmation. The app accesses only photos and documents you select through the Android system picker.

VIN decoding

When you request a lookup, the VIN is sent over an encrypted connection to the NHTSA vPIC service. NHTSA may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address. See the NHTSA privacy policy.

Google Play book purchases

Paid automotive books, manuals, checklists, and bundles are separate, one-time in-app purchases. Revive ’N Drive+ is a separate subscription and does not automatically include separately purchased Library products unless expressly stated.

Google Play processes payment. We do not receive or store full payment-card or bank-account details. Google Play may provide the product identifier, price or offer information, purchase status and time, a purchase token or account-linked purchase identifier, and acknowledgement, cancellation, refund, or revocation status.

The current app stores a one-way SHA-256 hash of the Google Play purchase token locally with the entitlement, not the raw token in the local purchase record. When Google sign-in is enabled, an eligible book entitlement may be associated with your signed-in account for restoration on supported devices. Signing in does not transfer purchase ownership to a different Google Play account.

Google Play purchase history is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

How we use information

  • Provide and personalize garage, maintenance, diagnostic, and Library features.
  • Save records and preferences you request.
  • Decode a VIN when you request it.
  • Process, verify, acknowledge, restore, and revoke digital entitlements.
  • Authenticate accounts and protect against unauthorized access.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.
  • Diagnose reliability or security problems and comply with law.

Sharing and disclosure

Limited information may be shared with Google for sign-in, Play Billing, restoration, fraud prevention, and Android services; with NHTSA when you request VIN decoding; with service providers needed for future account, sync, support, security, or hosting features; for legal and safety reasons; or during a business transfer subject to this policy and applicable law.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising SDKs or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Local garage data remains until you delete records, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Google Play retains purchase records under Google’s policies and applicable financial-record requirements.

If Google account features are enabled, request deletion of Revive ’N Drive account data by emailing tanneralcala@gmail.com with the subject Revive ’N Drive Data Deletion, preferably from the account email so ownership can be verified. We will delete or de-identify data we control except information required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record obligations. This does not delete your Google Account or Google Play history.

You may revoke Google access in your Google Account settings, decline Google sign-in unless an account-dependent feature requires it, decline camera access, and enter a VIN manually.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted network connections for supported requests and Android private app storage for structured local records. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general automotive audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided such information.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information we control, and to withdraw consent where applicable. We may need to verify identity before completing a request.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice in the app or app listing. Material changes apply prospectively unless otherwise stated.
