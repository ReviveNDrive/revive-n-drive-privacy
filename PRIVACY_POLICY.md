# Privacy Policy for Revive ’N Drive

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 22, 2026

Revive ’N Drive ("Revive ’N Drive," "we," "us," or "our") provides the **Revive ’N Drive: My Garage** Android application (package name `com.revivendrive`). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, when it is shared, and the choices available to you.

Revive ’N Drive is a consumer application for managing your own vehicles, maintenance and repair records, diagnostic notes, automotive reference information, and purchased digital books. It is separate from Mobile Mechanic Shop OS, which is a professional business product.

## Information we handle

### Google account information

If you choose to sign in with Google when account sign-in is available, Google may provide the app with basic account information that you authorize, such as your name, email address, profile image, and a Google account identifier. We use this information to identify your Revive ’N Drive account, associate eligible purchases and entitlements with you, restore access, and support account-related features.

We do not receive or store your Google password. Google handles the authentication process. You can review and revoke connected-app access through your Google Account settings.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

### Vehicle and garage information

Information you choose to enter may include:

- VIN, year, make, model, trim, engine, mileage, and vehicle category;
- vehicle notes and photos;
- maintenance and repair history, service dates, mileage, parts, costs, and notes;
- diagnostic symptoms, trouble codes, guided-diagnosis answers, observations, possible causes, and repair outcomes;
- saved part numbers, specifications, documents, receipts, and attachments; and
- app preferences and reading progress.

In the current version, this garage data is stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored records. If cloud backup or synchronization is introduced later, the app will identify that feature and this policy will be updated before cloud processing begins.

### Camera and selected files

Camera permission is optional and is used to scan text from a VIN plate, label, or document. Camera frames are analyzed on the device for text recognition. Revive ’N Drive does not use this feature to record video or continuously track you. A detected VIN is shown for confirmation before it is used.

When you select a vehicle photo, receipt, PDF, or other document, the app stores a reference that permits the app to display the selected item. The app accesses only files you select through the Android system picker.

### VIN decoding

When you deliberately request a VIN lookup, the VIN is sent over an encrypted connection to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration’s vPIC service so that vehicle details can be returned. NHTSA may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address as part of that request. See the [NHTSA privacy policy](https://www.nhtsa.gov/privacy) for information about NHTSA’s practices.

### Google Play purchases and digital books

Paid automotive books, manuals, checklists, and bundles are offered as separate, one-time in-app purchases. Revive ’N Drive+ is a separate subscription product and does not automatically include separately purchased Library products unless the app expressly says otherwise.

Google Play processes payments. We do not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. Google Play may provide the app with information needed to complete, verify, acknowledge, restore, and prevent fraud involving a purchase, including:

- the product identifier;
- price and offer information;
- purchase status and purchase time;
- a purchase token or an account-linked purchase identifier; and
- whether the purchase has been acknowledged, cancelled, refunded, or revoked.

The current app stores a one-way SHA-256 hash of the Google Play purchase token locally with the purchased product entitlement. It does not store the raw token in the local purchase record. Google Play purchase history remains subject to Google’s own retention practices and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

When Google sign-in is enabled, the app may associate an eligible book entitlement with your signed-in account so you can restore purchased access on supported devices. Signing in does not transfer ownership of a purchase to a different Google Play account.

### Technical information

Google Play, Google Identity, Android, and network services may process standard technical information necessary to provide their services, such as device and app version, IP address, request timestamps, diagnostic events, and transaction status. Revive ’N Drive does not currently include third-party advertising or behavioral advertising SDKs, and we do not sell personal information.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- provide and personalize garage, maintenance, diagnostic, and Library features;
- save the records and preferences you request;
- decode a VIN when you request that lookup;
- process, verify, acknowledge, restore, and revoke digital entitlements;
- authenticate accounts and protect them from unauthorized access;
- respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests;
- diagnose reliability or security problems; and
- comply with law and enforce applicable terms.

## When information is shared

We may share limited information:

- **With Google:** for Google sign-in, Google Play Billing, purchase restoration, fraud prevention, and Android platform services;
- **With NHTSA:** only when you request VIN decoding;
- **With service providers:** if needed to operate an account, cloud-sync, support, security, or hosting feature, subject to appropriate contractual safeguards;
- **For legal and safety reasons:** when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud, or respond to lawful process; or
- **During a business transfer:** if the app or its assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale, subject to this policy and applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Data retention and deletion

Locally stored garage information remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Google Play retains purchase records under Google’s policies and applicable financial-record requirements.

If Google account features are enabled, you may request deletion of the Revive ’N Drive account data associated with your Google sign-in by emailing **tanneralcala@gmail.com** with the subject **Revive ’N Drive Data Deletion**. Please send the request from the email address associated with the account so ownership can be verified. We will delete or de-identify account data that we control, except information we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record obligations. Deleting Revive ’N Drive account data does not delete your Google Account or Google Play transaction history.

You may also revoke Revive ’N Drive’s Google access through your Google Account’s connected-app settings. Revoking access stops future Google account access but does not automatically delete data already stored by the app; submit a deletion request if you also want that data removed.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted network connections for supported online requests and Android private app storage for local structured records. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children’s privacy

Revive ’N Drive is intended for a general automotive audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information we control. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To submit a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

You can decline Google sign-in unless a clearly identified account-dependent feature requires it. You can also decline camera access and enter a VIN manually.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app and legal requirements change. We will update the date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice in the app or through the app listing. Material changes apply prospectively unless otherwise stated.
