Delete your account and Garage data

You can delete your Revive 'N Drive account and its associated cloud Garage data either inside the Android app or by sending a verified deletion request.

Delete inside the app

Open Profile, find the Google account card, and select Delete Cloud Account & Garage. Confirm the request. A recent Google sign-in may be required for security.

Request deletion from the web

Email us from the Google email address connected to your Revive 'N Drive account. Use the subject Revive 'N Drive Account Deletion. This lets us verify ownership before deleting data.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

  • Your Revive 'N Drive Firebase Authentication account.
  • Cloud Garage data associated with that account, including vehicles, maintenance, repairs, saved parts/specifications, diagnostic history, notes, preferences, and reading progress.

What is not deleted

  • Your Google Account.
  • Google Play transaction history or the permanent Remove Ads purchase maintained by Google Play.
  • Records we must retain where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction obligations.

Local app data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Account deletion requests are processed after identity verification and within the period required by applicable law.
